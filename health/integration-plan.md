# Integration Plan: Keep Health + Papers + AI as a Minor Section

## Goal
Integrate your new health and papers content as a **secondary exploration track** without competing with your core brand (Physics + Quant + Software).

## Recommended structure
1. Keep top navigation unchanged.
2. Add a compact "Explorations" section to `index.html` (homepage, below Writing and above Books).
3. Link three destinations:
   - `health/index.html` (Health Lab)
   - `health/papers.html` (Papers)
   - `health/ai-projects.html` (AI Projects)
4. Do **not** create a primary top-nav item unless this content becomes a major portfolio pillar.

## Files already created
- `health/index.html`
- `health/papers.html`
- `health/ai-projects.html`
- `health/styles.css`

## Why this works
- Preserves professional positioning for recruiters and admissions readers.
- Shows breadth and curiosity without diluting your main narrative.
- Gives AI projects a clear but measured framing: applied experiments.

## AI Projects recommendation
Yes, add AI projects. Present them as **Applied AI Experiments** with a strict template per project:
- Objective
- Stack/architecture
- What was measured
- Result/limitation

This keeps credibility high and avoids a generic "AI" label.
