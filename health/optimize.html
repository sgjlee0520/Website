High-value execution model

Learning mechanics

Active recall + spacing + interleaving

Prioritize retrieval and spaced review over re-reading to maximize retention per hour.

Volume constraint

4 hours of real deep work per day

Treat this as the quality ceiling; add shallow/admin work separately without confusing the two.

Training integration

Lift hard, schedule smart

Do not place hardest study blocks immediately after intense lifting due to transient cognitive fatigue.

Cardio requirement

2-3 weekly sessions

Zone-2 or short intervals to support BDNF, mood stability, and memory function.

Practical weekly blueprint

Use this as a base template and adjust by academic load.

Daily study

2-3 deep blocks

Run 90-minute blocks with strict breaks and one retrieval-heavy review window.

Weekly totals

~24h deep + ~12h shallow

Track quality metrics, not just time logged.

Sleep

Consistent 7-9h window

Anchor wake time and protect pre-sleep environment for memory consolidation.

Exam prep

Taper before performance

Reduce volume 24-48h pre-exam; prioritize retrieval and calm-state execution.
