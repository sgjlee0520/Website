Speed of Sound

The speed of sound in air from time of flight. 비행 시간으로 측정한 공기 중 음속.

PHYS 20BL Oscilloscope Weighted fit

Overview개요

PHYS 20BL, Winter 2025. I measured how long sound takes to reach a microphone at five distances between 81 and 121 cm, reading the arrival time on an oscilloscope three times at each distance.

A weighted linear fit of distance against time gives the speed from the slope: 341.0 ± 0.6 m/s. The notebook also works through the residuals, a chi-squared check and a t-test against the expected 343 m/s.
