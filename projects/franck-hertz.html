Franck-Hertz Experiment

Measuring the first excitation energy of mercury from inelastic electron collisions. 비탄성 전자 충돌로 수은의 첫 들뜸 에너지를 측정했습니다.

PHYS 20CL Atomic physics Data analysis

Overview개요

PHYS 20CL, Spring 2025. I accelerated electrons through mercury vapor and recorded the collected current as I swept the accelerating voltage. The current dips at regular intervals. Each dip is where electrons have gained just enough energy to excite a mercury atom in an inelastic collision, and so lose that energy.

The average spacing between dips was 5.03 V, which gives a first excitation energy of 5.03 eV for mercury.
