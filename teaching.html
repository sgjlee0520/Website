A study of studying with AI (PHYS 180)

I took PHYS 180, Teaching Physics, the same quarter. For the course project I asked whether studying with AI pushes students toward the way novices organize physics knowledge, and whether they notice after the exam.

I surveyed the PHYS 23 class three times, anonymously: 103 responses before the midterm, 63 after it and 102 before the final, out of about 105 students. I also ran a problem-sorting task adapted from Chi et al.

  • 88% used AI to study for the midterm.
  • 48% recognized the concept on a problem but could not start the setup, and 68% still felt ready.
  • Frequent self-testing fell from 48% to 26% after the midterm.
  • The sorting task was inconclusive, because it was confounded.

I presented the study at the end of the quarter. The responses were collected with survey-gate, a small page I wrote that asks the survey questions before it releases the practice exam.
